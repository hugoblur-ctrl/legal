📄 Terms of Use

Effective Date: August 12, 2026
App: Outpost — 지구정복
Developer: Beomseok Jeong

1. What the app does

Outpost turns the places you spend time into territory on a map. Staying in one place claims it; returning on different days fortifies it. Claiming the map is free and always will be — there is no limit on how much of it you can fill.

2. Please look up from your phone

Do not use this app while driving, cycling, or crossing a road. Outpost is designed to work with the screen off, so there is never a reason to hold it while moving. You are responsible for your own safety and for obeying local laws. Do not enter private property, restricted areas, or anywhere unsafe in order to claim tiles.

3. Purchases

Themes change the appearance of the map only. They do not affect how fast or how far you can claim.

  • Individual themes are one-time purchases. Once bought, a theme is yours permanently.
  • All Access is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that unlocks every theme, including seasonal editions released while you are subscribed.

4. Subscription terms

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • If the subscription ends, themes you were using through All Access are locked again. Themes you purchased individually remain yours.

5. Refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests are made to Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com; the developer cannot issue refunds directly.

6. Your data

Your map is stored on your device. The developer does not receive it and therefore cannot restore it for you. See the Privacy Policy.

7. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to falsify location data, tamper with the app, or use it in a way that breaks the law. Doing so may result in loss of access to optional online features.

8. Disclaimer

The app is provided "as is". Map data, district boundaries, and reachable-area estimates are approximations and may be inaccurate. The app is a game and must not be relied on for navigation, emergency use, or any purpose where an error could cause harm.

9. Changes

These terms may be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date above and described in the app's release notes.
