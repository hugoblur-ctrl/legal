✅ No Account · No Tracking

Effective Date: July 17, 2026
App: Cast — 낚시 패턴 파인더 (Fishing Pattern Finder)
Developer: Beomseok Jeong

1. Overview

Cast is designed with privacy as a foundation. The app does not run any account system or backend that identifies you, and it does not track you. Your catch logs and fishing spots are stored on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No sign-up: we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • No usage analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No contacts, photos library, or health data

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your catch logs, fishing spots, and the environmental snapshot saved with each catch (tide, moon phase, pressure, time of day) are stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). The developer has no access to them and they are not uploaded to any server run by the developer.

4. Location

With your permission, Cast uses your location while you are using the app to save a fishing spot at your current position and to look up the conditions for that point. Your location is stored on your device as part of the spots you create.

Location permission is optional. If you decline it, you can still add and use fishing spots by choosing them on the map yourself. You can change or revoke the permission at any time in your device's Settings.

5. Weather Data (Third Party)

To show weather, pressure, and forecasts for a spot, the app sends that spot's coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com) and receives the forecast back. These are plain read-only requests: no account, no login, and no user identifier is sent, and the developer does not receive or log them. Open-Meteo handles those requests under its own privacy policy.

6. Tracking & Advertising

Cast does not track you and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. No third party receives your catch logs or your spots.

7. Purchases (Subscription)

The optional subscription (Cast+) is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles all payment details; the developer does not collect or store your card or payment information.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for ages 4 and up and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app removes all associated data. You may also delete individual catches and spots within the app at any time.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the Effective Date above will be updated.

3. 기기 내 저장 데이터

조과 기록, 낚시 포인트, 그리고 조과와 함께 저장되는 환경 정보(물때·월령·기압·시간대)는 Apple의 기기 내 저장소(SwiftData, UserDefaults)를 사용해 이용자 기기에만 저장됩니다. 운영자는 이 데이터에 접근할 수 없으며, 운영자가 운영하는 어떤 서버로도 전송되지 않습니다.

위치 권한은 선택 사항입니다. 허용하지 않아도 지도에서 직접 지점을 선택해 포인트를 추가·이용할 수 있습니다. 권한은 기기 설정에서 언제든지 변경하거나 철회할 수 있습니다.
