Why we keep asking

Twelve is the floor, not the finish line

Google wants to see that a real group used the app for two weeks. Twelve people is where they start looking — not where they stop. And the count has to hold for 14 days straight: if someone uninstalls partway through, the clock goes back to zero and we start over. So every extra person is what actually gets these across the line.

No. We never ask for payment details.
