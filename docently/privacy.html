✅ No Ads · No Cross-App Tracking

Effective Date: August 1, 2026
App: Docently — A Painting a Day
Operator: Kim Yewon (김예원)

Docently ("the App") respects your privacy and processes the minimum data necessary to provide the app's features.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or address.

  • Anonymous usage data: to power daily viewing, streaks, and the personal gallery, we store viewing activity (which artworks you viewed and completed) and your viewing streak, linked only to an anonymous identifier that is not tied to your real identity.
  • Optional sign-in: if you choose to sign in (Sign in with Apple on iOS, or sign-in on Android), we receive a stable account identifier supplied by the platform so that your streak and saved artworks can be restored across your devices. We do not receive or store your email address or password.
  • We do not access location, contacts, photos, microphone, or other sensitive device data.

2. Storage & Processing

Anonymous usage data is stored and processed via our cloud database provider (Supabase). Artwork images are served via Cloudflare R2. Some settings — such as your preferred language and locally cached streak — are stored on your device.

3. Payments

Subscriptions (Docently+) are processed through Apple App Store In-App Purchase on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. Payment method and card details are handled by Apple or Google respectively; the App does not collect, receive, or access your payment information.

4. Tracking & Advertising

The App shows no advertising and performs no third-party or cross-app tracking (no IDFA on iOS, no advertising ID on Android).

5. Third Parties

Infrastructure: Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudflare (image delivery via R2). Payments: Apple (App Store), Google (Google Play). We do not sell or share your data with any other third party.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

To request deletion of the anonymous usage data associated with your account or device, please contact us at the email below. We will process your request within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted at this URL, and the Effective Date above will be updated.
