✅ No personal data collected · No ads · No tracking

Effective Date: October 3, 2026
App: SCRAP SMASH
Developer: Cabotine

1. The short version

SCRAP SMASH is an offline action game. We do not collect personal data, and the app does not send your gameplay to us. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress is saved on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

2. What the app stores

  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded to a server operated by us. This version has no online features.

3. What the app does not include

  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The game is built with Unity. Unity's optional services (analytics, ads, crash and performance reporting) are turned off in this app.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

4. Google Play and Apple

When you download the app, the store you use (Google Play or the App Store) processes data under its own privacy policy. Test builds distributed through Google Play testing tracks may show us aggregate crash and performance statistics that Google provides; these do not identify you to us.

5. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example cloud save or analytics), we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.

1. 요약

SCRAP SMASH는 오프라인 액션 게임입니다. 개인정보를 수집하지 않으며, 게임 기록을 저희에게 보내지 않습니다. 계정·광고·추적이 없습니다. 진행 상황은 기기에만 저장되며 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워집니다.

2. 앱이 저장하는 것

  • 게임 진행 상황과 설정은 기기에만 저장됩니다.

3. 앱에 들어 있지 않은 것

  • 위치·연락처·카메라·마이크·사진에 접근하지 않습니다.

5. 아동

앱은 아동을 포함한 누구의 개인정보도 의도적으로 수집하지 않습니다.
